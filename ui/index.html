    The bank — not the importer — funds the escrow: it needs a TradeUSD allowance for the core (use Approve TradeUSD (max) above while connected as the bank). The named exporter can ship only after the credit is issued and operative.

    4 · Presentation, examination & custody

    Present only after the stipulated document set (★ below) is anchored and the goods are loaded — the B/L is part of every presentation. The bank re-examines on-chain validity before honoring: sight pays immediately; deferred schedules payment at honoredAt + deferral.
